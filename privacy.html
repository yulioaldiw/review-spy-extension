Privacy Policy for ReviewSpy AI

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

Last Updated: October 19, 2026

ReviewSpy AI ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Google Chrome Extension handles information when you use our service.

1. What Data We Collect

ReviewSpy AI follows a strict Zero-Telemetry, Privacy-First Architecture:

  • Customer Reviews Data: When you click "Analyze Competitor Reviews", the extension reads publicly accessible review content on the active Amazon or Shopify tab in your browser. This data is processed temporarily in memory to generate AI insights.
  • API Keys: If you provide a Google Gemini API Key, it is stored locally on your device in your browser's private storage (chrome.storage.local). We do not collect, transmit, or have access to your personal API keys.
  • License Keys: If you activate a Pro subscription, your license key is verified securely via HTTPS against our payment merchant (Lemon Squeezy) and cached locally on your device.
  • Zero Analytics / Tracking: We do NOT use third-party analytics trackers, cookies, or fingerprinting scripts. We do NOT collect your browsing history, personal identity, or search queries.

2. How We Use Information

Any data accessed by ReviewSpy AI is used solely to:

  • Perform AI analysis of competitor reviews as explicitly requested by you.
  • Export parsed reviews to CSV format upon your command.
  • Verify your subscription status for feature access.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your data to any third party. Review data is transmitted solely to:

  • Google Generative AI (Gemini): Sent encrypted over HTTPS to produce competitor summaries and ad hook suggestions.
  • Lemon Squeezy: Used strictly for license verification if you choose to activate a Pro subscription.

4. Data Storage and Retention

All settings, cached analyses, and API credentials reside strictly in your local browser storage. You can delete all cached data and keys at any time via the "Clear Cached Analyses" button in the extension Settings tab or by uninstalling the extension.

5. Chrome Web Store User Data FAQ Compliance

ReviewSpy AI complies with the Google Chrome Web Store Single Purpose and User Data Policies. We only request permissions necessary to deliver the core functionality:

  • sidePanel: To show competitor analytics beside the product listing.
  • storage: To save your settings and local cache on your computer.
  • activeTab: To extract public review text only from the tab you explicitly inspect.
